The problem we are solving

Starting a business is expensive. Meanwhile, useful equipment sits unused in other homes.

The barrier

Many women want to start a tailoring, baking or craft business but cannot afford a new sewing machine, oven or tools. Buying everything new can delay or stop a dream before it begins.

The HerCycle solution

A simple platform where people list equipment and materials they no longer need — and where women entrepreneurs can find them at a fraction of the cost, and see exactly how much they save.

How it works

Three simple steps from idea to income.

1

Discover

Search and filter reusable equipment and materials near you.

2

Compare costs

Use the savings calculator to see whether renting or buying is cheaper.

3

Start earning

Express interest, get your business going and track the impact of reuse.

Why HerCycle?

One platform, three kinds of impact.

FinTech

Transparent rent-vs-buy calculations and savings estimates so every rupee is an informed choice.

Sustainable Tech

Extends the useful life of existing equipment and materials instead of creating new waste.

Women Empowerment

Lowers the cost of entrepreneurship and helps women build income-generating businesses.

How this is calculated
  • Total rental cost = Monthly rental cost × Number of months (+ any extra rental costs you entered)
  • Potential savings from renting = Purchase cost (+ any extra buying costs) − Total rental cost
  • Deposits, transport, maintenance, resale value and other costs are not included unless you enter them under optional extra costs.

Assumptions behind these numbers

  • Starting values are demo data: 14 reused resources and ₹18,500 saved are made-up sample figures.
  • Waste diverted: we assume an average of 3 kg of equipment or material per reused resource. This is a rough illustrative assumption, not a measured value.
  • Money saved: for simulated transactions, savings = cost of buying new − amount paid on HerCycle (never below ₹0).
  • We do not claim any carbon-emission reductions.
